How these are built. 🌼 Every number pulls from the Total column of the Inbound Opp – All Members export, which is already filtered to Associated Partner = Scale Computing. Total # of leads sums Record Count across the 46 campaigns in scope. Total CPL divides total Sum of Actual Cost in Campaign ($975,930) by that lead count; CAC divides the same cost by total Sum of Won Opportunities in Campaign (439 closed-won deals) instead. Cumulative ROI value of opps and ROI closed come from Sum of Value Opportunities in Campaign and Sum of Value Won Opportunities in Campaign.

Why the Total column, not a sum of the five quarters. 🌈 Cost and ROI value read as current snapshot figures per campaign — the same dollar amount repeats across every quarter a campaign had active members, then drops once it closes out — so adding the five quarter columns together would double- and triple-count campaigns that span more than one quarter. The exported Total column already accounts for that and is the correct cumulative figure; Record Count is the only metric here that's genuinely additive across quarters.

Top 4 campaigns, last quarter. 💐 Ranked by ROI closed per dollar of Sum of Actual Cost in Campaign within the Q3 CY2026 column only — the last fully completed quarter, since Q4 CY2026 started today. Campaigns with zero recorded spend that quarter are excluded from this ranking (return per dollar isn't defined for them), even where they drove plenty of leads or pipeline; the full quarterly table below has those too.

Not in the headline numbers: ✨ the export also includes Sum of Value Opportunities (Soft/Ser) ($54,988,091 total) — soft/services-attributed opportunity value. Say the word if you want that added as a tile.

The new cuts, in plain terms: 🦋 overall ROI multiple and open pipeline both use the same Total-column figures as the headline tiles, just recombined. Lead-to-customer rate and average deal size divide total won opportunities and total closed value by leads and won opps respectively. The organic/paid split and the spend-with-no-return list both key off whether a campaign's Sum of Actual Cost in Campaign (Total column) is zero or not, same basis as everything else on this page.
